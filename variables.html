Variable definitions

Names and definitions of every variable in the World Fiscal Uncertainty downloads.

Naming convention

Variable names are built from three parts: view + measure + variant, followed by a suffix for the scale.

View (first letter)

  • W world
  • D domestic press
  • X foreign press
  • A all sources

Measure

  • FU fiscal uncertainty
  • FA fiscal attention
  • EPU economic policy unc.
  • MPU, TPU monetary, trade
  • SDU sovereign-debt unc.
  • UNC any uncertainty

Suffix (scale)

  • none index, 2016–2025 = 100
  • _Z standardized log share
  • _SH share per 1,000 articles

Variant tags sit between the measure and the scale suffix: lexicon _UB, _EB, _FN, _FB; construction _NOE, _FOC, _INT; sample _PNL, _NAT, _OWN, _ENG; timing _FY, _PB. For example, DFU_FB_Z is the standardized domestic fiscal uncertainty with the broad fiscal lexicon, and XEPU_PNL is the foreign-press economic policy uncertainty index on the stable outlet panel.

The column Files shows where each variable appears: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual.
