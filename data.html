Data

World and country fiscal policy uncertainty indices in Excel and CSV format, with metadata.

The data may be used for research and teaching, provided that the paper in which the indices are constructed is cited: Balcilar, M. (2026), Global Uncertainty and Fiscal Space: Who Can Still Afford to Act? (PDF); see Citation and terms of use.

Formats

Each data set is provided as an Excel workbook and as a CSV file. The first sheet of each workbook (Metadata) records the source, the construction, the version and the citation, and defines every variable; the second sheet (Data) contains the series. Each CSV file is accompanied by a metadata CSV file with the same content. This is version 1.0, released in October 2026, with GDELT data up to 30 September 2026. The year 2026 is incomplete (January–September) and is flagged in the annual files (PARTIAL = 1).

World indices

The world files contain one row per period. Daily files are indexed by DATE (YYYY-MM-DD) and by YEAR, MONTH and DAY; weekly files by the ISO-8601 YEAR and WEEK, with WEEK_START (Monday) and WEEK_END (Sunday); monthly files by YEAR and MONTH; quarterly files by YEAR and QUARTER; and annual files by YEAR.

The principal variable is WFU, the World Fiscal Uncertainty index (2016–2025 average = 100), accompanied by WFU_Z (standardized logarithm of the share), WFU_SH (fiscal uncertainty articles per 1,000 articles) and the article counts N_W and K_W. The files also contain the lexicon and construction variants of the world index, its stable-outlet version WFU_PNL, and the companion series WEPU, WEPU_NF, WMPU, WTPU, WSDU, WUNC and WFA. In the daily file, LOWVOL = 1 marks days on which GDELT recorded fewer than 100,000 articles or no articles at all (29 August 2017 and 15 June – 1 July 2025).

Country indices

The country files are long panels with one row per economy and period, identified by ISO3 (ISO 3166-1 alpha-3) and COUNTRY, and by YEAR and MONTH (monthly), YEAR and QUARTER (quarterly) or YEAR (annual). The panel is balanced over January 2016 – September 2026; a cell without articles is missing rather than zero.

The headline files contain the series required for most applications:

XFU, XFU_Z, XFU_SH Foreign fiscal uncertainty, from the press of other countries writing about the economy

FUGAP_Z Foreign-minus-domestic gap, XFU_Z − DFU_Z

DFU_FY, DFU_PB (annual) Domestic index on the IMF WEO fiscal-year basis and over the six pre-budget months

The full files add every variant used in the robustness analysis of the paper: the broad and narrow lexicons; the versions without the economy requirement and restricted to focused coverage; fiscal uncertainty intensity and fiscal attention; the native-language, own-country and English-language domestic press; stable-panel versions of all views; the fiscal-year and pre-budget bases for the three views; and the companion economic policy, non-fiscal economic policy, monetary policy, trade policy and sovereign-debt uncertainty series and the tone of fiscal coverage. Each variable is defined under Variable definitions.

Notes on use

  • In regression analysis the standardized series (*_Z) are recommended: one unit is one within-economy standard deviation of the log share, which makes coefficients comparable across economies whose press differs in size.
  • The indices (2016–2025 mean = 100 in every economy) are intended for figures.
  • Comparisons of levels across economies should use the shares (*_SH).
  • Thin cells (THIN_D = 1) should be excluded or down-weighted, and the stable-panel series DFU_PNL used where COMPBRK_D = 1.

Construction

The article counts are computed from GDELT on Google BigQuery and aggregated with the code used in the paper; the monthly and annual country files reproduce the data of the paper to numerical precision. The quarterly, weekly and daily files apply the same procedure to quarterly, weekly and daily sums of the same counts. The procedure is summarized under Methodology and documented in Appendix B of the paper.
